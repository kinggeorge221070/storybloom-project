FAQ

Everything you need to know before you order

How long does the process take?

Most projects are completed within 7–12 working days after payment and content approval, depending on format and revisions.

Can I include personal photos?

Yes. You can upload multiple photos, select a preferred cover image, and arrange them within the story layout.

Do you offer guest checkout?

Absolutely. You can order as a guest or create an account at any stage.

Can I edit the AI-generated content?

Yes. The content is reviewed and can be revised before approval for print.

Do you support mobile money and cards?

Yes. The platform is designed for Ghana-friendly payment methods, including mobile money and card payments where enabled.
